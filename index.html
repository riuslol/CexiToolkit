Vana’diel time

—
Every Abyssea NM gets three random weaknesses when you claim or pop it. Hit each colour once for the best rewards; repeating the same colour shortens the effect and drops the bonus. A weakness can’t be triggered while the NM is already staggered, stunned, casting, or using a TP move.

🔵 Blue — physical weapon skill

Set by the game time you claimed it. One of these 15 — boosts equipment & weapon drops, and inflicts Amnesia.

🔴 Red — elemental weapon skill

One of these 13, chosen at random. Secures Atma, Abyssite & key items, and inflicts Terror.

🟡 Yellow — magic

The spell’s element matches the previous, current or next Vana’diel day — so check all three columns. Boosts synthesis & Empyrean armour drops, and inflicts Silence.
